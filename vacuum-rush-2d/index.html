How to Play

Move with WASD or ↑←↓→. You drive the vacuum — it does not drive itself.

Battery drains while you move. Bag fills as you suck up dirt. Both are fixed at the charging dock — park on it to recharge and empty out.

Furniture blocks you. Slamming into it at speed costs time, battery, and your combo.

Stairs end the run instantly. Spills short you out for 2 seconds — no control until it passes.

Power-ups: ⚡ speed, ✚ double suction, ◆ shield (ignores hazards).

Hit the level's target clean % then return to the dock and press E to finish early for a time bonus. P pauses, R restarts the level.
