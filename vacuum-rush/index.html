How to Play

Drive it yourself — it does not drive itself. In the default chase camera, W/S drive forward and reverse and A/D steer, exactly like a real robot vacuum. Press V for the overview camera, where WASD moves screen-relative instead.

Battery drains while you move. Bag fills as you suck up dirt. Both are fixed only at the charging dock — park on it to recharge and empty out.

Furniture blocks you. Slamming into it at speed costs time, battery, and your combo.

Stairwells end the run instantly. Spills short you out for 2 seconds — no control until it passes.

Power-ups: ⚡ speed · ✚ double suction · ◆ shield (ignores hazards).

Hit the target clean % then return to the dock and press E or Enter to finish early for a time bonus.

Free camera: drag anywhere to look around, scroll to zoom, Q/E to swing, C to recentre, V to switch between chase and overview.
